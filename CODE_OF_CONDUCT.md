# Code of conduct

Be direct and be kind. Review the work, not the person. Disagreements about a verdict are settled with sources and versions, not volume. Harassment of any kind gets a warning once and removal after that. Report problems to support@wellworn.dev.

This is a short form of the Contributor Covenant 2.1; its full text applies: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
